01
Surface tech — 94–97% grit retention

Raw carbon is out; four durable grits take over

Traditional raw carbon wears fast (John Kew's cannon test strips 20–30% of its spin; on Pickleball Effect's rig it keeps only 81% of its roughness on average). Every paddle topping November's charts uses a new hardened surface: 11SIX24 HexGrit (about 1% lost over 75 games for STS), Honolulu Crystal Blue (97% retained for Pickleball Effect), Six Zero Diamond Tough Pro and B&B Space Dust.

02
Core tuning — from raw power to feel

Gen 4 foam enters the era of split feel

Going by Pickleball Effect's and John Kew's feel classifications, November's popular new paddles fall into two camps: the crisp, poppy Stiff & Hollow camp of the Loco 2, J2CR and Power 2, and the softer, pocketing camp of the Volair CMD, Six Zero Coral Pro and RPM Jade. With durable grit now common, the question has shifted from which paddle hits hardest to which feel suits you.

03
Two-track rules — USAP vs. UPA-A certification

USAP starts direct spin testing at 40 mph (2,100 RPM cap)

Since October 1, USAP measures spin off a 40 mph shot with dual high-speed cameras. The Honolulu Crystal Blue, 11SIX24 Power 2 and Volair CMD currently hold UPA-A approval only; for USAP-sanctioned events, the B&B Loco 2, Spartus Spitfire, RPM Jade and the dual-certified Six Zero Coral Pro are the compliant picks (the Paddletek Veyra is currently UPA-A approved only).

為什麼同一支球拍，
會有不同結論？

2026 年 11 月最常出現「A 頻道說塗層超耐磨、B 頻道說轉速掉了 15%」的現象，核心原因在於各家的磨耗測試方法與手感偏好截然不同。以下拆解本期引用的五個主要評測來源。

1

John Kew Pickleball

物理實驗室派

主持 KewCast・與 RPM 共同開發 Q2 球拍・建立 KewCOR 能量回彈與 Feel Map 手感資料庫

核心武器

03 — Authority Reviewers Methodology

Why does the same paddle get different verdicts?

This November, one channel keeps calling a grit "indestructible" while another reports a 15% spin drop. The reason: each source measures wear differently, and each prefers a different feel. Here are the five main sources this issue draws on.

1

John Kew Pickleball

The physics lab

Hosts KewCast · co-developed the Q2 with RPM · built the KewCOR energy-return and Feel Map databases

Method
An air-cannon accelerated wear test: 10 averaged shots at 60 mph, 100 shots at 70 mph on one spot, then 10 more averaged shots, rated Tier 1 (0–5% spin loss) to Tier 4.
Blind spots
The cannon measures single-point impact wear, which doesn't always match an abrasion rig or real play. The Spitfire lost about 14% in his test (Tier 3), while Pickleball Effect's abrasion rig rated it Tier 1.
Signature pick
Six Zero Coral Pro Hybrid (Tier 1, Power band)
2

Pickleball Studio

Chris Olson · Long-haul play-testing

An independent reviewer rated around 4.7 DUPR · 400+ paddles tested since 2021, each with at least 10 hours of court time · caps his commissions at 15% and calls out big brands

Method
Long stretches of real play, plus his own measurements of swing weight, twist weight and spin (e.g., 2,423 RPM on the RPM Jade).
Perspective
Prizes sweet-spot stability and soft-game feel; gave the original Loco a 9/10 as one of the most balanced full-foam paddles, and praised the original Boomstik's huge sweet spot.
Signature pick
B&B Loco (9/10) / Selkirk Boomstik (original)
3

Pickleball Effect

Braydon Unsicker · Abrasion rig & tuning school

Quantifies power, pop and firepower as percentiles · his Tuning Lab studies how lead tape changes swing and twist weight

Method
A lab abrasion rig with controlled wear cycles and a profilometer measuring how much Ra and Rz roughness survives; raw carbon averages 81% Ra retention.
Perspective
Doesn't rank paddles against each other; groups picks by playing style, labels each paddle's feel quadrant (Stiff / Soft × Hollow / Dense), and flags USAP or UPA-A limits.
Signature pick
B&B Loco 2 (4.9/5) / 11SIX24 Power 2 Ultré (best durability)
4

Matt's Pickleball

Matt Khoury · Big data + measured database

Publishes a weekly chart of what players research on his site · has measured 600+ paddles on the same equipment

Method
A Briffidi swing/twist weight rig, a radar gun for serve and volley speed, a high-speed camera at over 500 frames per second for spin, and a stylus roughness tester.
Perspective
A long-term tester: 30 hours on the Jade before a verdict, 50 hours tracked on the Spitfire. In his picks of the year he's torn between the RPM Friction Pro V2 and the Volair CMD.
Signature pick
Volair CMD / RPM Friction Pro V2 / Spartus Spitfire (best value)
5

Pickleheads

Brandon Mackie & Stephen Mijangos · Tiered play-testing

Together they've tested 200+ paddles, each with at least 10 hours of real games before scoring

11/9 winners
  • Best overall #1: Honolulu J2CR Crystal Blue ($175.50)
  • Best intermediate #2: Bread & Butter Loco 2 ($198)
  • Best budget #3: Vatic Pro V-SOL Tour Evergrit ($99.99)
  • Best control #7: Paddletek Tempest Veyra ($162)
Other awards
  • Best power: RPM Q2 ($212.50 with code)
  • Best lightweight: Volair CMD ($162)
  • Most durable: 11SIX24 Ultré Power 2 (about $200)
  • Best pro: JOOLA Pro V ($299.95)

為什麼三家權威測 Spitfire，

Spartus Spitfire（PermaGritNano）開箱轉速各家公認頂尖，但三種耐磨測試卻得出三種結論。

PICKLEBALL EFFECT · 實驗室磨耗儀

The hot debate · The Spitfire Rashomon

Why did three top reviewers get three different results?

Everyone agrees the Spartus Spitfire (PermaGritNano) spins at the top of the market out of the box, yet three durability tests reached three different conclusions.

I.
PICKLEBALL EFFECT · LAB ABRASION RIG

Tier 1: virtually no loss

After mechanical abrasion it kept 94% Ra and 97% Rz (vs. 81% / 77% for raw carbon), the best grit retention he's tested on a $100 paddle.

II.
JOHN KEW · CANNON IMPACT TEST

Tier 3: moderate wear (about −14%)

100 shots at 70 mph on one spot cost both units about 14% of their spin: Tier 3. Better than raw carbon's 20–30%, but not elite.

III.
MATT'S PICKLEBALL · LONG-TERM PLAY

Wears with play, but stays high-spin

Roughness fell 14–15% after 30 hours and about 19% after 50. Starting from 2,347 RPM, it remains in high-spin territory after the drop.

THE VERDICTThe three tests measure different kinds of wear: Tier 1 on an abrasion rig, 14–19% lost after impacts and long play. The Spitfire isn't zero-wear, but at $99 it's still one of the strongest spin picks you can buy.

鉛片／鎢絲膠帶配重：三個基本原則

3 & 9 點鐘
（拍面兩側）
適合：J2CR / CMD / Jade 這類扭重偏低的輕拍

PB Effect 的建議：加在 3、9 點鐘最能提高扭重、擴大橫向甜區，揮重增加最少。J2CR 這類原廠扭重偏低的拍，加鉛效果特別明顯。

拍頭（12 點鐘附近）
適合：想要更多穿透力的快手型球友

加在越遠離握把的地方，揮重增加越多：抽球與發球更有份量，但網前手速會變慢。建議每次 1–2 克慢慢加。

原廠直上免配重
（Stock Ready）
適合：11SIX24 Ultré P2 / Paddletek Veyra

Ultré 扭重 6.7–6.8，屬 Hybrid 前四分之一；Veyra 內建周邊阻尼桿，Pickleheads 測試時完全不需要加重。開箱即有高容錯。

Pro Tuning Guide

Lead and tungsten tape: three basic rules

3 & 9 o'clock
(the sides)
For: light, low-twist paddles like the J2CR / CMD / Jade

Pickleball Effect's advice: weight at 3 and 9 raises twist weight and widens the sweet spot side to side with the least swing-weight cost. On low-twist paddles like the J2CR, the difference is especially noticeable.

The tip (near 12 o'clock)
For: fast-handed players who want more plow-through

The farther from the handle you add weight, the more swing weight goes up: heavier drives and serves, but slower hands at the net. Add 1–2 grams at a time.

Stock, no lead
(Stock Ready)
For: 11SIX24 Ultré P2 / Paddletek Veyra

The Ultré's 6.7–6.8 twist weight is in the top quarter of hybrids, and the Veyra's built-in perimeter damping rods meant Pickleheads never needed to add weight. Very forgiving straight out of the box.
