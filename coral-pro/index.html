開篇 · 忍術三大奧義Opening · The three secret arts

先別怪天分，
先看懂你手上的拍子Before you blame talent,
read your paddle

新手最常卡在兩個地方：底線抽球越用力越出界，網前對攻時拍子像綁了鉛塊，怎麼樣都慢半拍。這兩件事，技術占了大半；但球拍確實會放大，或縮小你的弱點。實驗室用三個數字來描述這件事：轉速（Spin）、揮拍重量（Swing Weight）與扭轉重量（Twist Weight）。看懂它們，你就知道一支拍子能幫你什麼，又幫不了什麼。Beginners get stuck in two places. The harder they drive, the further the ball sails long; and in kitchen-line exchanges the paddle feels chained to an anvil, always a beat late. Technique is most of the fix — but a paddle can magnify or shrink a weakness. Labs describe that with three numbers: spin, swing weight and twist weight. Learn to read them and you'll know what a paddle can do for you, and what it can't.

「球拍不會替你練球；但選錯拍，會讓你的弱點更明顯。」“A paddle won't practise for you. The wrong one just makes your weak spots louder.”
本刊觀點Our view

I轉速 · 下墜魔球Spin · the dipping ball

是什麼What it is
RPM，球每分鐘轉幾圈。RPM — how many times the ball turns per minute.
為什麼重要Why it matters
上旋會讓球往下彎（馬格努斯效應）。同樣的出手速度，上旋越強，球越早落地——這就是「用力抽也不出界」的原理。Topspin bends the ball downward (the Magnus effect). At the same pace, more topspin brings the ball down sooner — which is how a hard drive still lands in.
它做不到的事What it can't do
拍面只是幫手。轉速主要來自你由下往上「刷」的揮拍路徑；把球平平推出去，再粗的拍面也轉不起來。The face only helps. Most spin comes from a low-to-high brushing swing; push the ball flat and no amount of grit will spin it.

II揮拍重量 · 神速拔刀Swing weight · the quick draw

是什麼What it is
揮動時感受到的「重」，也就是繞握把轉動的慣量，不等於秤上的重量。How heavy the paddle feels in motion — its rotational inertia around the handle — not what it weighs on a scale.
為什麼重要Why it matters
數值越低，網前（Kitchen line）快攻時拍子越快到位。The lower it is, the faster the paddle gets into place when a speed-up comes at the kitchen line.
代價The trade-off
揮重低的拍子，擋重球時比較不紮實，抽球也少一點「推送感」。快和沉，很難兩者兼得。A low swing weight feels less solid blocking heavy balls and gives drives less plow-through. Quick and heavy rarely come together.

III扭轉重量 · 絕對防禦盾Twist weight · the shield

是什麼What it is
球沒打在甜區時，拍子抵抗在手中翻轉的能力。How well the paddle resists twisting in your hand when you miss the sweet spot.
為什麼重要Why it matters
數值越高越寬容，有效甜區越大：偏一點的球，拍面不容易歪，球還是能穩穩過網。Higher means more forgiving and a bigger effective sweet spot: on slightly off-centre hits the face holds its angle and the ball still clears the net.
它做不到的事What it can't do
真的打到拍框，任何拍子都救不了。抗扭幫的是「偏一點」，不是「沒打到」。Hit the frame and no paddle will save you. Twist weight helps with a near miss, not a miss.

讀懂實驗室 · 本期資料來源Reading the lab · Our sources

四個來源，
四把不同的尺Four sources,
four different rulers

每個實驗室的儀器和測法都不同，數字只能和「同一個來源」互相比較。本刊以 Matt's Pickleball 為主要標準（三種拍型的資料最完整，2026 年 11 月仍在更新），其餘來源用來交叉驗證。Every lab uses different instruments and methods, so numbers only compare within the same source. We use Matt's Pickleball as our standard — it has the fullest data on all three shapes and was still being updated in November 2026 — and use the others to cross-check.

  1. 01

    Matt's Pickleball 本刊標準Our standard

    揮重用 Briffidi SW1、抗扭用 Briffidi TWA2 量測；轉速以每秒 500 格以上的高速攝影機判讀；另用測速槍測發球與推擋截擊球速，並量拍面粗糙度。每個數字都附上在他資料庫中的百分位。Swing weight on a Briffidi SW1, twist weight on a Briffidi TWA2, spin read from a high-speed camera at over 500 frames per second, plus radar-gun serve and punch-volley speeds and face roughness. Every figure comes with its percentile in his database.

  2. 02

    John Kew 原簡報來源The deck's source

    轉速用 Stalker Pro 3s 雷達槍量真人揮拍，超過 2,300 RPM 列為 Elite；KewCOR 用砲彈機以 50 MPH 打 LT 球量拍面回彈（高於 0.39 屬力量型）；另做 100 次高速撞擊後的轉速耐用度測試。Spin from a Stalker Pro 3s radar gun off a real human swing — above 2,300 RPM is Elite. KewCOR fires an LT ball at 50 MPH to map rebound (above 0.39 is the Power band). He also retests spin after 100 high-speed impacts for durability.

  3. 03

    Pickleball Effect

    Braydon 的球拍資料庫。轉速數字普遍比 Matt 低約 400 RPM（測法不同），另外追蹤拍面粗糙度在磨耗後保留多少。Braydon's paddle database. Its spin numbers read roughly 400 RPM below Matt's (a different method), and it tracks how much face roughness survives wear.

  4. 04

    Six Zero 原廠規格Six Zero factory specs

    官網標示的揮重、抗扭與尺寸。這是品牌自己的數字，不是第三方實測，所以只拿來對照。The swing weight, twist weight and dimensions on the brand's store. They're the maker's own figures, not independent tests, so we use them for reference only.

實驗室 RPM ≠ 比賽認證的 RPMLab RPM is not certification RPM

USA Pickleball 自 2026 年 10 月 1 日起，對新送審的球拍設下 2,100 RPM 的轉速上限；職業端的 UPA-A 從 2024 年就用同樣的上限。Coral Pro 是通過 UPA-A 認證的球拍，Matt 卻量到 2,485 RPM——因為認證機構用的是自己的發球機與測法。所以，別拿評測網站的 RPM 判斷一支拍子合不合法，要查官方名單。Since 1 October 2026, USA Pickleball has capped newly submitted paddles at 2,100 RPM; the pro-side UPA-A has used the same cap since 2024. The Coral Pro is UPA-A approved, yet Matt measured 2,485 RPM — because certifiers use their own cannon and protocol. So don't judge legality from a review site's RPM; check the official list.

同一支拍，轉速從 2,079 到 2,485 RPM，差了 406。這不代表誰量錯，而是測法不同；跨網站比數字，等於拿公分和英吋比長短。The same paddle reads anywhere from 2,079 to 2,485 RPM — a 406 RPM gap. Nobody measured wrong; the methods differ. Comparing across sites is comparing centimetres with inches.

    5. 5

      新手要知道的副作用A side effect beginners should know

      Matt 提醒：這麼「咬球」的拍面，連打偏的球都會加轉，放小球可能飄、平擊發球會多跳一點，通常練一兩次就能適應。Matt's caveat: a face this grabby adds spin even to mishits, so loose drops can float and flat serves kick more than expected. It usually takes a session or two to adjust.

    1. 1
      越用力越出界 → 上旋抽球階梯Drives sail long → topspin ladder從廚房線開始，每退一步連續 10 球，由下往上刷，目標落在底線前一大步。退到底線還能控住，才加速。Start at the kitchen line; at each step back hit 10 in a row with a low-to-high brush, landing a big step inside the baseline. Only add pace once you can hold it from the baseline.
    2. 2
      網前手太慢 → 廚房線快手對攻Slow at the net → kitchen-line hands拍頭朝上、拍子放在胸前，雙方連續 20 球不掉；動作只用手腕和前臂，不准大拉拍。Paddle tip up, paddle in front of your chest, 20 balls in a row with your partner. Wrist and forearm only — no big backswings.
    3. 3
      打偏就翻拍 → 過渡區重置Face twists on mishits → transition resets同伴從後場抽球，你站在過渡區，用放鬆的握拍把球擋進廚房。握得越緊，拍面越容易被打歪。Your partner drives from the back; you stand in the transition zone and soak the ball into the kitchen with a relaxed grip. The tighter you squeeze, the more the face gets knocked off line.
