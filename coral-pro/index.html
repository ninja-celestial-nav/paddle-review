開篇 · 忍術三大奧義Opening · The three secrets

三個數字，決定你在場上的樣子Three numbers decide how you play

磅秤上的重量幾乎說明不了什麼。真正決定手感的，是球拍動起來之後的表現。The number on a scale tells you almost nothing. What matters is how the paddle behaves once it moves.

I · Spin

下墜魔球The dipping ball

轉速是球拍表面摩擦帶動球旋轉的次數。轉速越高，上旋球越早往下掉——你可以放心用力揮，球還是會落在界內。Spin is how fast the face makes the ball rotate. More topspin makes the ball dip sooner, so you can swing harder and still land it in.

II · Swing weight

神速拔刀The quick draw

揮拍重量是揮動時感受到的「重」，不等於秤上的重量。數值越低，網前（Kitchen line）快攻時拍子越快到位；越高，擊球越有份量，但反應會慢一拍。Swing weight is how heavy the paddle feels in motion, not on a scale. Lower means faster hands in kitchen-line exchanges; higher means more plow-through but a slower reaction.

III · Twist weight

絕對防禦盾The shield

扭轉重量是沒打中甜區時，球拍抵抗翻轉的能力。數值越高，容錯率與甜區感越大，打到拍框附近也比較不會在手中翻轉。Twist weight is how well the paddle resists rotating on off-center hits. Higher means a more forgiving, larger-feeling sweet spot.

痛點一 · 越用力，球越飛？Pain point 1 · Hit harder, ball sails long?

明明想打到底線，球卻出界。高轉速的上旋讓球提早下墜——Matt's Pickleball 測得 Widebody 2,485 RPM，是他資料庫中的第 100 百分位。You aim for the baseline and the ball sails out. Heavy topspin pulls it down earlier — Matt's Pickleball measured the Widebody at 2,485 RPM, the 100th percentile in his database.

痛點二 · 網前對峙，手速太慢？Pain point 2 · Too slow in hand battles?

對方快攻時，拍子像綁了鉛塊。Widebody 實測揮拍重量 108.75（第 14.4 百分位），屬於偏輕、好甩的一端。When the speed-up comes, your paddle feels like lead. The Widebody measured 108.75 swing weight (14.4th percentile) — at the light, quick end.

痛點三 · 一打偏就翻拍？Pain point 3 · Paddle twists on mishits?

扭轉重量 7.25（第 92.7 百分位）。就算腳步沒跟上、用靠近拍框的位置擋球，拍面也比較不會翻。Twist weight 7.25 (92.7th percentile). Even when your feet are late and you block near the edge, the face is less likely to turn.
